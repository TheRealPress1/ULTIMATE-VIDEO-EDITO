# Finding ideas

An idea is ready to script when it has a thesis (what the viewer needs) and a want (what makes them stop scrolling). Ideas come out better in batches than one at a time.

## Need versus want

| Viewer | Need: becomes the thesis | Want: becomes the opener |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult guitar beginner | Short daily practice instead of long weekly sessions | To play one song at a friend's party |
| Freelance illustrator | A written scope before any sketch | To stop doing a third round of free revisions |
| Renter in a big city | Talking to owners directly | €250 a month back |

The viewer usually doesn't know about the need, and wouldn't click on it if they did. "Write a scope document" is a lecture. "How I stopped doing free revisions" is a video. The opener sells the want, and the body delivers the need.

## Five sources

| Source | Where to look in Blackout | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The creator's turning points | Ask, or `footage_search` past interviews for "the moment", "that's when", "I realized" | Thesis |
| The viewer's false beliefs | The dossier in [audience](audience.md) | Thesis |
| Results the viewer envies | The dossier; the creator's own before/after numbers | Opener |
| Questions people keep asking | Comments and DMs the user pastes (for example into `research/comments.md` in the project); the same question asked twice in interview transcripts (`footage_search`) | Both |
| Outliers | The creator's own posts with at least twice their usual views. `instagram_posts` gives no median, so work it out: the median views of the latest 25 (default `sortBy: "publishedAt"`) is the usual, and the top 25 from `sortBy: "views"` are the candidates. Leave out posts whose views are null. Reference videos the user supplies count too | Opener and format |

An outlier tells you a topic or an opener landed once. It doesn't tell you why. Pull one variable from it, such as the topic, the opening line or the format, and test that.

## Two ways to sharpen an idea

- **The result after the result.** A plain result is what the method produces. The result after it is what that unlocks in the viewer's life. "Save €250 on rent" becomes "keep the flat with the balcony and still travel in August". Ask "and then what?" until the answer makes the viewer picture something.
- **One level down.** A surface problem has a cause underneath, and the cause is usually the better video. "I can't stay consistent with practice" sits on top of "every routine I find assumes a free hour, and I have eight minutes". Ask "why?" until the answer is specific to this viewer.

Write fresh examples in the creator's niche every time. The examples on this page show the move. Don't use them as content.

## Run a batch

1. Set a timebox: fifteen minutes in chat, or one reply.
2. Fill quotas: five ideas from each source, 25 in all. Don't judge while generating.
3. Rank the top three to five, each with a one-line reason under three tests:
   - **Proof exists.** The creator has the number, the story or the footage, or can get it.
   - **It's specific.** A picture forms when you read the title.
   - **It fits a format.** You can name its beat sheet from [formats](formats.md).
4. Offer the top picks. Script one only when the user chooses it.

## Idea records

Keep each idea as one line, in chat or under `## Ideas` in a brief:

```
- Free revisions: thesis: scope before sketch · want: stop the third round · source: question · format: belief-flip · proof: have (client email, May) · status: chosen
```

Use one of these statuses: `new`, `chosen`, `scripted`, `filmed`, `posted`.
